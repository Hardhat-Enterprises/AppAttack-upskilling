# Quick Startup in WSTG

**Starter kit for web security testing**

## Why I made this

When I first started learning web application security testing, I didn't really know where to start. I didn't have a strong background in web security, and I wasn't confident about what to look for or how to approach testing an application.

I hope the following material helps you out in some way, whether it's to move forward or solidify your current baseline of skills and knowledge.

This repository is a collection of resources that helped me get moving, including:

* YouTube videos and walkthroughs
* Useful tools and references

The key for me has been **exposure**. The more I was exposed to different ideas, tools and examples, the easier it became to understand what I was looking at and what questions I should be asking.

I'm still learning, and I don't expect that to stop. Hopefully, this helps overcome the **"where the hell do I start?"** hurdle or exposes you to other tools and techniques you can experience for yourself.

**You don't need to know everything before you start. You need enough exposure to start figuring out what you don't know.**
